# If I Lead a Business One Day — Profit or Purpose?
### A 6–7 minute speech in two opposing perspectives · 中英对照（精简版）

> **Delivery.** ~900 words, ≈6:30 at 140 wpm with pauses. Beats: Opening → **A: Profit First** → **B: Purpose First** → **Evil Case: Enron** → **Synthesis**. `[ ]` = stage direction, don't read aloud. Bold lines land — slow down.
>

## Opening (0:00–0:30)

If I lead a business one day — profit, or other goals? It's easy to *answer*, hard to *mean*. So I'll give two honest answers, then tell you which I actually believe.

**中文：** 如果有一天我做企业——优先利润，还是其他目标？这问题容易回答，却很难真正算数。所以我给两个诚实的答案，再说我真正信哪个。

---

## A — Profit First (0:30–2:30)

Profit is not greed, and not the enemy of purpose. **Profit is oxygen** — you can have all the purpose you want, but without it you're just a beautiful idea that goes bankrupt.

Three reasons. **One: profit lets purpose survive time.** Amazon spent nearly a decade barely breaking even, plowing everything into logistics — had it demanded a profit every quarter, it could never have served hundreds of millions of people cheaply. **Two: the market is a truth-teller.** Ryanair is disliked by its own passengers, yet it's one of Europe's most profitable airlines, because it delivers the one thing people chose it for: cheap, reliable flights. **Three: profit is what lets me compete at all.** A company that pays workers best and uses the cleanest materials has higher costs; if it can't profit, it loses to the cheaper rival who doesn't care — and then the people I meant to protect lose their jobs anyway. **A business that dies of its own virtue saves no one.**

**中文：** 利润不是贪婪，也不是目标的对立面。**利润是氧气**——目标再多，没有它，你也只是个破产的“美丽想法”。

三个理由。**一，利润让目标活过时间。**亚马逊近十年几乎不盈利，把钱投进物流——若它每个季度都要利润，根本不可能让几亿人低价购物。**二，市场说真话。**瑞安航空被自家乘客嫌弃，却是欧洲最赚钱的航司之一，因为它兑现了人们真正要的东西：便宜、准点。**三，利润是竞争资格。**给工人最好待遇、用最干净材料的公司成本更高；赚不到钱就输给更便宜的对手——到头来我本想保护的人也照样失业。**一个死于自身美德的企业，谁都救不了。**

---

## B — Purpose First (2:30–4:30)

But the profit-first case hides one assumption: that **profit measures value created.** It doesn't. It measures value *captured* — and the gap between those words is where disasters live.

**Purdue Pharma.** OxyContin made the Sacklers billions — a "successful" business by any quarterly measure. But profit is computed *after* you decide what to ignore, and what Purdue ignored was that the drug was addictive. Nearly 645,000 Americans died of opioid overdose; Purdue went bankrupt, paid about $7.4 billion, and was barred from selling opioids. **Profit measured the money captured, not the harm created.**

Profit is a good servant, a terrible master. Make it the *goal* instead of the *gauge*, and people game it — Wells Fargo's staff opened millions of fake accounts to hit targets. **Any metric that becomes the target stops being a good measure.**

And the counter-case the profit side never mentions: **Patagonia** built its business around environmental purpose and has been profitable for decades; **Novo Nordisk** prioritized drugs over short-term margin and became Europe's most valuable company. **A company is not an engine for money; it's a promise, and profit is how you keep it.**

**中文：** 但“利润优先”藏着一个假设：**利润衡量被创造的价值。**并不是——它衡量被*攫取*的价值，两者的落差正是灾难所在。

**普渡制药。**奥施康定让萨克勒家族赚了几十亿，按任何季度标准都是“成功”。可利润是在你决定忽略什么之后才算的，而普渡忽略的，是这种药会上瘾。近 64.5 万美国人死于阿片过量；普渡破产、赔付约 74 亿美元、被禁售。**利润衡量的是攫取的钱，不是造出的伤害。**

利润是好仆人、坏主人。把它当*目标*而非*仪表*，人们就去博弈它——富国银行员工为冲指标开了数百万假账户。**任何指标一旦变成目标，就不再是好指标。**

利润率方从不提的反证：**巴塔哥尼亚**围绕环保做生意，几十年一直盈利；**诺和诺德**优先研发药物而非短期利润，成了欧洲市值最高的公司。**公司不是赚钱的引擎，而是一个承诺，利润是兑现它的方式。**

## The Evil Case — Enron (4:30–5:30)

To see profit-first with the purpose stripped all the way out, look at **Enron**. Named "America's Most Innovative Company" six years running — on a story manufactured with accounting tricks and hidden debt. When the truth broke in 2001, it collapsed in weeks. Thousands lost jobs *and* retirement savings; investors lost around $74 billion; the executives had already cashed out, and the auditor Arthur Andersen died with it.

Enron didn't merely *fail* to prioritize purpose — it made **deception the business model**, and it worked quarter after quarter, until it didn't. When the number is the only thing that matters, lying about the number becomes rational. **Evil, in business, rarely announces itself — it just optimizes.**

**中文：** 想看把目标抽干后“利润优先”能变成什么样，就看看**安然**。连续六年“美国最具创新力公司”，靠的是会计手段和隐藏债务编出的故事。2001 年真相曝光，几周内崩塌：数千人失业又丢退休金，投资者损失约 740 亿美元，高管早已套现，审计方安达信随之覆灭。

安然不是“没优先考虑目标”——它把**欺骗本身做成了商业模式**，一个季度一个季度奏效，直到崩盘。当数字是唯一重要的事，谎报数字就成了理性策略。**在企业里，恶很少自报家门——它只是一味地优化。**

---

## Synthesis (5:30–6:30)

So which one? **"Profit or purpose" is a false choice — that's exactly why it's dangerous.** People who pose it are selling an extreme.

The real question isn't *which* to prioritize — it's **how long is my horizon?** Over one quarter, purpose is just a cost. Over ten years, trust compounds, employees stay, and the harm you externalized comes home. That's **Toyota**'s lesson: "lean" looked wasteful short-term, yet made it the world's most reliable — and most profitable — carmaker.

So I'd **use profit as the instrument and purpose as the standard** — and build in accountability by design, not just my own good intentions, which are worth little under pressure.

My final answer: **I will prioritize profit — because I intend to still be here in ten years, and doing good is the only thing that compounds that long.**

Thank you.

**中文：** 那到底哪个？**“利润还是目标”是假二选一——正因如此才危险。**把问题说成二选一的人，都是在推销某个极端。

真问题不是*优先哪个*，而是**我的时间尺度有多长？**一个季度里，目标只是成本；十年里，信任复利、员工留下、你外部化的伤害会回家。这正是**丰田**的教训：精益短期看像浪费，却让它成为最可靠、也最赚钱的车企。

所以我会**把利润当工具、把目标当标准**——并把问责制度化，而不是只靠自己的善意（压力面前，善意不值钱）。

我的最终答案：**我会优先利润——因为我打算十年后还活着，而做好事，是唯一能复利这么久的事。**

谢谢。
